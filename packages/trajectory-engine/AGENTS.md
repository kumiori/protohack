# trajectory-engine working agreement

## Origin and purpose

This package emerged from experiments in constructing trajectories and
collective timelines without erasing uncertainty, disagreement, or authorship.
It represents intended paths and realised histories so they can be compared and
coordinated without manufacturing consensus.

## Invariants

Preserve: influence != direction != topology; plan != realisation; uncertainty
remains uncertainty; qualitative time remains valid; history is append-only;
branches are explicit; authorship remains attributable; comparison != consensus;
sharing != joining; and persistence != publication.

Before extending the package ask whether the change preserves independent
meaning or collapses distinct trajectories merely to simplify an interface. If
it collapses meaning, reject or redesign it.
