# A Protocol for the Commons

This is AI bullshit. `> Yes, but read it through. Read it twice, at least.` Is there a leap, if so, where?

### What's been the real success of the Web as we know it?

#### Everyone could independently publish. Everyone could independently link. Nobody needed permission. The protocol itself generated network effects.

Today every AI interaction is largely isolated.

Human > AI model > Answer > Human

The chain ends there. But consequences are global.
None of the value produced seems reusable. Almost none becomes attributable. Let alone discoverable. None of that work becomes composable, _compostable_.
The energy dissipates. And everything warms. Fast forward 2026, try enjoying southern Europe in August, without _air conditioning._

How many of these interactions are "for others", for the "commons", How to know it?
How much energy is `wasted`? How much energy is goint into the commons?

### A possible open charter

Drafting the opening paragraph of a future Working Group:

The Stewardship Protocol defines interoperable data structures and interaction patterns for representing shared necessities, contributions, attribution, verification, and closure across distributed human and artificial intelligence systems. Its goal is to ensure that value generated through collective action remains discoverable, attributable, reusable, and recursively beneficial to the commons.

### Philosophy

We are attempting something radical:

> to construct the conditions under which an interior determination can pass into objective reality, become collective action, encounter its consequences, and return to itself as an enlarged capacity for freedom.

### 13. The (strong) formulation of the protocol

> The protocol enables a situated necessity to gather and expose its conditions, encounter difference and contingency, mobilise attributed capacities, pass into accountable action, and return through verified consequences as an enlarged common capacity.

Purpose:

> To prevent the energy generated by human–AI cooperation from terminating in private extraction, untraceable decision-making or isolated outputs, by making its entire movement through knowledge, labour, power, action and consequence visible and recursively available to the commons.
